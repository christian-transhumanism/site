AI could transform the world.
Or end it.

The most powerful tool humanity has ever built is in our hands. What we do with it will define everything that comes after.

Section I

What we stand to lose

AI without wisdom is not progress. It is peril. And the risks are no longer science fiction.

  1. Concentrations of power

    Wealth, knowledge, and force collapsing into the hands of a few — with no democracy able to check what no human can fully understand.

  2. Mass surveillance and manipulation

    Free societies hollowed out from within. Every choice steered by systems we cannot see.

  3. Engineered pathogens

    Diseases designed in hours, not years.

  4. Misaligned superintelligence

    Vast capability indifferent to human flourishing.

This is not a future worth having.

Section II

What we are called to build

Guided by wisdom and the deepest aims of the human spirit, AI could help us heal, restore, and reach further than ever before.

  • Heal the body

    "the lame walk, the blind see"

    AI accelerating drug discovery and diagnosis — helping eliminate diseases that have plagued humanity for centuries.

  • End poverty

    "good news to the poor"

    AI improving agriculture, logistics, and access to expertise — helping lift people out of poverty.

  • Ease suffering

    "he will wipe away every tear"

    AI making mental health care accessible and effective, easing chronic and invisible suffering.

  • Extend life

    "life, and life abundantly"

    AI advancing longevity and aging research — helping add years of health and vitality.

  • Renew the earth

    "the renewal of all things"

    AI modeling climate, restoring ecosystems, and reviving endangered species.

  • Cultivate the cosmos

    "fill the earth"

    AI enabling space-faring — autonomous probes, smarter missions, the long work of carrying life beyond Earth.

"Behold, I make all things new."

This is the future Christianity has always called us toward.
